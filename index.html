About Us – Amporia Works

At Amporia Works, we believe innovation begins with accessibility. We are a student-led initiative with a dedicated shop inside our university, created to make essential electronic components and technical services easily available to students, hobbyists, and researchers.

Our mission is to empower creators by providing a one-stop shop for high-quality electronics, CAD assistance, prototyping services, and more. Whether you're building your first circuit or developing a complex project, Amporia Works is here to support your journey from concept to creation.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to quality, affordability, and customer satisfaction. Our team is passionate about technology and innovation, and we strive to foster a community where ideas can flourish and creativity knows no bounds.

Driven by a passion for hands-on learning and engineering excellence, we aim to make innovation more accessible, practical, and impactful for our university community.
